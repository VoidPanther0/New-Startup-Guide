# Step 5 — Competitor (Rival) Analysis

**Goal:** Analyze competitors (rivals), find weaknesses and bugs.

## Process

1. Google your shortlisted keywords from Step 2.
2. Open the **top 5–10 results** (your competitors/rivals).
3. For each competitor, note:
   - What they do well.
   - **Weaknesses** (slow, ugly UI, missing features, outdated, paywalled, no mobile).
   - **Bugs** you can reproduce (broken links, mobile issues, confusing UX, errors, 404s).
4. Look for gaps: features users ask for in reviews/Reddit but competitors lack.
5. Decide your **differentiator**: faster, simpler, free, better UX, or niche focus.

## Deliverable

A competitor table:

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | Bugs found | Gap / Your differentiator |
|------------|-----------|------------|------------|---------------------------|
| ...        | ...       | ...        | ...        | ...                       |

Plus your chosen differentiator.
