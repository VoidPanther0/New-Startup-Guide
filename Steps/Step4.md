# Step 4 — Build with AI (Write the Code)

**Goal:** Use AI to write the website code.

## Process

1. Describe your problem, features, and differentiator to AI.
2. Let AI generate the code (HTML/CSS/JS or a framework).
3. Review, test, and fix bugs.
4. Make sure the site works **before** buying the domain.
5. Then connect your chosen `.com` domain (from Step 3).

## Deliverable

A working website, ready to go live on your `.com` domain.
