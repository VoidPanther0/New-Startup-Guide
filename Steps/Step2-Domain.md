## Step 3 — Find & Choose a Domain (.com)

**Goal:** Find a `.com` domain that contains your main keyword. Buy it last.

### Tool

- Instant Domain Search: https://instantdomainsearch.com/

### How to find

1. Take your main keyword from Step 2 (example: "Font Finder") — or a golden keyword
   from Step 6 if you already found one.
2. Search it on https://instantdomainsearch.com/.
3. The domain **must be `.com`**.
4. **Google suggest trick:** In Google, type your keyword + a space. Google
   automatically suggests the next word. Press space after the keyword and Google
   suggests more keywords. Combine a suggestion to form a great domain.
   - Example result: `fontfinderai.com`
5. Choose the best available `.com` (short, memorable, keyword included).

### Rules

- The **main keyword** must appear in the domain name.
- **Only `.com`** domains.
- The product (tools) will be built on this domain.
- Keep it short, memorable, easy to spell.
- Avoid hyphens and numbers if possible.

### Important

**Buy the domain only after the website is built (Step 4).** If you buy a domain
with no website, the domain and money are wasted.

### Buying tips

- **Sirf `.com` lo** — `.com` hi SEO aur trust ke liye best hai.
- ⚠️ **Renewal price dekho, first-year price nahi** — ₹99 first year dekh ke kharidna
  phir ₹1200/yr renewal aata hai. Cart mein "renewal price" pe click karke check karo.
- **WHOIS privacy free** rakho (Namecheap / Spaceship / Hostinger mein by default free hai).
- **Auto-renew ON** karo — expire ho gaya domain recover karna mushkil hota hai.
- **2FA ON** karo (registrar ke account mein).
- **Alag email se register karo** — personal Gmail mat use karo, ek dedicated email banao.
- **Hosting Cloudflare rahegi** — registrar sirf naam rakhta hai, hosting alag hai.
- 💡 Nameservers Cloudflare wale rakhne hain (next step) → phir **Cloudflare Registrar**
  (at-cost, zero markup) se kharidna sabse sasta padta hai.
