## 1.1 Find the Problem

**Goal:** Find real problems people actually have, so your website solves something valuable.

### 1. Find Your Own Problems

- Write down problems you personally face daily/weekly.
- Look at your own workflow, tools you use, things that annoy you.
- Ask yourself:
  - "What do I wish existed?"
  - "What takes too much time?"
  - "What do I keep Googling again and again?"
- If you face it, others likely face it too.
- **Tip:** keep a running note of problems for 7–14 days before picking one.

### 2. Reddit & Quora

- Search Reddit and Quora for complaints and questions:
  - "how do I", "is there a tool", "alternative to", "frustrated", "annoying".
- Subreddits to start: `r/SaaS`, `r/webdev`, `r/smallbusiness`, plus niche-specific subs.
- Sort by **Top** / **This year** to find recurring pain points.
- Collect the exact phrases people use — these become your keywords later.
- Upvotes + comments = demand signal.

### 3. Make it Micro

- Don't solve everything; pick a **micro** problem.
- Narrow down to **one specific audience + one specific pain**.
- Micro = smaller scope, faster to build, easier to rank.
- Example: instead of "CRM for everyone" → "CRM for freelance photographers".

### Deliverable

A list of **5–10 problem statements** (one sentence each), ready for **1.2 Is it Worth it?**

## 1.2 Is it Worth it? (Idea Verification)

**Goal:** Verify the idea before building. Use free keyword tools.

### Tool

- Free Keyword Generator: https://ahrefs.com/keyword-generator

### Process

1. Take the problem phrases from **1.1 Find the Problem**.
2. Enter them into the Ahrefs free keyword generator.
3. Check the **search volume** for each idea.
4. **Make sure volume is around 100 and 1000** (target band: ~100 to ~1000 monthly searches).
   - Too low (< 100): not enough demand.
   - Too high (> 1000): too competitive for a new site.
5. Look for keywords with clear intent:
   - tool, calculator, generator, checker, template, converter, formatter.
6. Shortlist **3–5 keywords/ideas**.

> Note: "100 and 1000 volume" means the monthly search volume should sit in the
> 100–1000 range — the sweet spot for a new website.

**Important:** Do not buy a domain yet. You will *choose* the domain in **Part 2 — Domain**,
but buy it only after the website is built in **Part 3 — Build** — buying a domain with no
website wastes money.

### Deliverable

Shortlisted ideas, each with: **keyword + estimated volume + intent**, ready for **1.3 Competitor Analysis**.

## 1.3 Competitor (Rival) Analysis

**Goal:** Analyze competitors (rivals), find weaknesses and bugs.

### Process

1. Google your shortlisted keywords from **1.2 Is it Worth it?**
2. Open the **top 5–10 results** (your competitors/rivals).
3. For each competitor, note:
   - What they do well.
   - **Weaknesses** (slow, ugly UI, missing features, outdated, paywalled, no mobile).
   - **Bugs** you can reproduce (broken links, mobile issues, confusing UX, errors, 404s).
4. Look for gaps: features users ask for in reviews/Reddit but competitors lack.
5. Decide your **differentiator**: faster, simpler, free, better UX, or niche focus.

### Deliverable

A competitor table:

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | Bugs found | Gap / Your differentiator |
|------------|-----------|------------|------------|---------------------------|
| ...        | ...       | ...        | ...        | ...                       |

Plus your chosen differentiator.

## 1.4 Traffic Checker & Golden Keyword

**Tool:** https://ahrefs.com/traffic-checker/

### Process

1. Go to Ahrefs Traffic Checker.
2. Enter the **competitor domains** from **1.3 Competitor Analysis**.
3. Check:
   - How much traffic each site gets.
   - **Which keywords** they rank for.
4. Make sure to look at **pages 3–4** (not just top results):
   - If **Reddit or government (.gov) websites** appear on pages 3–4 for a keyword → that's a **GOLDEN keyword**.
   - Golden keyword = low competition opportunity.
5. **Note which keyword belongs to which website** — everyone focuses on the top sites,
   so the remaining keywords have **lower competition**.
6. Collect golden keywords — these are your final target keywords.

### Deliverable

A list of **golden keywords** with competition notes and the website each came from.
