# Step 1 — Find the Problem

**Goal:** Find real problems people actually have, so your website solves something valuable.

## 1. Find Your Own Problems

- Write down problems you personally face daily/weekly.
- Look at your own workflow, tools you use, things that annoy you.
- Ask yourself:
  - "What do I wish existed?"
  - "What takes too much time?"
  - "What do I keep Googling again and again?"
- If you face it, others likely face it too.
- **Tip:** keep a running note of problems for 7–14 days before picking one.

## 2. Reddit & Quora

- Search Reddit and Quora for complaints and questions:
  - "how do I", "is there a tool", "alternative to", "frustrated", "annoying".
- Subreddits to start: `r/SaaS`, `r/webdev`, `r/smallbusiness`, plus niche-specific subs.
- Sort by **Top** / **This year** to find recurring pain points.
- Collect the exact phrases people use — these become your keywords later.
- Upvotes + comments = demand signal.

## 3. Make it Micro

- Don't solve everything; pick a **micro** problem.
- Narrow down to **one specific audience + one specific pain**.
- Micro = smaller scope, faster to build, easier to rank.
- Example: instead of "CRM for everyone" → "CRM for freelance photographers".

## Deliverable

A list of **5–10 problem statements** (one sentence each), ready for Step 2.
