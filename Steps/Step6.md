# Step 6 — Traffic Checker & Golden Keyword

**Tool:** https://ahrefs.com/traffic-checker/

## Process

1. Go to Ahrefs Traffic Checker.
2. Enter the **competitor domains** from Step 5.
3. Check:
   - How much traffic each site gets.
   - **Which keywords** they rank for.
4. Make sure to look at **pages 3–4** (not just top results):
   - If **Reddit or government (.gov) websites** appear on pages 3–4 for a keyword → that's a **GOLDEN keyword**.
   - Golden keyword = low competition opportunity.
5. **Note which keyword belongs to which website** — everyone focuses on the top sites,
   so the remaining keywords have **lower competition**.
6. Collect golden keywords — these are your final target keywords.

## Deliverable

A list of **golden keywords** with competition notes and the website each came from.
