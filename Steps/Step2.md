# Step 2 — Is it Worth it? (Idea Verification)

**Goal:** Verify the idea before building. Use free keyword tools.

## Tool

- Free Keyword Generator: https://ahrefs.com/keyword-generator

## Process

1. Take the problem phrases from Step 1.
2. Enter them into the Ahrefs free keyword generator.
3. Check the **search volume** for each idea.
4. **Make sure volume is around 100 and 1000** (target band: ~100 to ~1000 monthly searches).
   - Too low (< 100): not enough demand.
   - Too high (> 1000): too competitive for a new site.
5. Look for keywords with clear intent:
   - tool, calculator, generator, checker, template, converter, formatter.
6. Shortlist **3–5 keywords/ideas**.

> Note: "100 and 1000 volume" means the monthly search volume should sit in the
> 100–1000 range — the sweet spot for a new website.

**Important:** Do not buy a domain yet. Build the website first (Step 4); buying a
domain with no website wastes money.

## Deliverable

Shortlisted ideas, each with: **keyword + estimated volume + intent**, ready for Step 3.
